Past events:

- Speaker Events   
  - Animation club has hosted large scale events such as:   
    - A trip to Lightbox convention   
    - A speaker panel with Sam Register (the president of warner bros animation)   
    - A speaker panel with the animators from Teen Titans Go   
    - A speaker panel with Magery Dean   
- Weekly workshops or socials   
  - We have hosted weekly animation workshops covering topics from the 12 principles of animation to life drawing to character design.   
  - Animation Club also hosts fun socials such as movie screenings\!   
- Animation Jam   
  - We have hosted a summer and winter animation jam\! The summer animation jam had artists from across the country have a month to create an animated short film with their team.   
- Multi Animator Project (MAP)   
  - Our annual multi-animator project is an animated music video that has artists animating their own unique clip in each section\!
